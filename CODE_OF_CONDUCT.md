# Code of Conduct

GovHawk welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and
experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and focused on the technical work.
- Assume good intent while addressing ideas and evidence directly.
- Protect sensitive data. Use synthetic examples and redact environment details.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope, safety, and the single-file
  runtime constraint.
- Give credit for ideas, testing, documentation, and code contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
publication of private information, and attempts to introduce malicious code or
sensitive data are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's abuse-reporting controls for urgent platform safety concerns. For a
project conduct concern, contact the repository owner through a private channel
listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not place private or sensitive details
in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this policy. Repeated or serious violations may lead
to temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is
officially representing GovHawk.
